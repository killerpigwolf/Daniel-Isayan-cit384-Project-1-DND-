Session notes

Based on the updated campaign notes.

Grouped by events; the original notes have no session dates.

FeloaRedwoodCatacombsHobgoblin campThe arena

Feloa & the missing miners

  • We begin in Feloa's mining country. The miners have not returned. The council wants us to restore the mines and restart operations.
  • Meet the city warden. Solving the problem earns a reward and the council's favor.
  • Lysethia oversees the miners. We meet her at the throne with an imposing knight.
  • We gather at a keep to cash in.
  • Crimson defeats Lysethia in a spar with help from the others.

Redwood & the orb

  • Council member Redwood wants power and is turning people into lycanthropes.
  • We attempt to attack him and steal back the orb. We recover it and go on the run.
  • Our mission is to take down Redwood.
  • Crimson and Phyllarcice have nightmares in the woods because of their lycanthropy.

Cressida & the catacombs

  • We meet Cressida, an older fighter.
  • In the catacombs, we find a little beholder.
  • Oswaldo makes a contract with it to help defeat Redwood.

The village & hobgoblin camp

  • A man digging a grave knows Redwood.
  • A drow passed through the burned-down village: tall, lanky, pointed nose, very sunken eyes.
  • Aldren Redwood is the son. Samuel Redwood is the father.
  • Cressida comes with us from the village.
  • We defeat the hobgoblin camp and hold a drow hostage to escape.
  • The drow work for King Phusgloth. He is in the north and works with the Redwoods and the hobgoblins.

Next objective

Alexandria & the arena

  • Alexandria, a higher-up in the southern capital, can find information about the mysterious orb Phyllarcice carries.
  • To speak to her, we must defeat an archmage or another formidable opponent in the gladiator arena.
  • In the south, problems and questions get settled with fists.

Names to remember

Lysethia
Noble in charge of the miners.
Redwood
Council member creating lycanthropes.
Aldren & Samuel
The Redwood son and father. Which one is the council member is still unclear in the notes.
Cressida
Fighter traveling with us. Also spelled Cresida.
Phusgloth
Northern drow king allied with the Redwoods and hobgoblins.
Alexandria
Our contact in the southern capital, if we win in the arena.
