Background

A follower of Selune who seeks redemption through helping others, while trying to keep a curse from burdening the people around her.

Campaign notes

Phyllarcice and Crimson suffer nightmares in the woods because of their lycanthropy. She carries the mysterious orb the party recovered from Redwood.

Alexandria in the southern capital may have answers about the orb. The party must first win a fight in the gladiator arena to meet her.

Also written as Phyliarces and Phylarcies in the session notes.
