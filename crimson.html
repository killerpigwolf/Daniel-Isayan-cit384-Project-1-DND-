Background
Soldier

Bjorgvin

At seven, Crimson survived a drow attack on his village near Winterfall. Winter Crow took him into Bjorgvin, a snow elf tribe, and raised him as his own son.

At nine, he defended his friend Bjorn against a starving wolf pack. Winter Crow gave him the name Crimson Wolfe. The fight left him scarred and afraid of wolves.

Leaving home

At seventeen, Crimson left to find his own purpose. The tribe gave him plate armor and a fur cloak. Winter Crow gave him a magical wolf-crested helmet that had belonged to his late son.

Crimson still carries his mother's dagger and worships Shevarash. He rarely takes his helmet off in public and prefers action to conversation.

Evelyn Ravenwood

A cleric named Zaythamore introduced Crimson to Winterfall and its guard. Crimson was assigned to Evelyn, the youngest Ravenwood necromancer, and later became her personal knight. They grew close through training and eventually began a secret relationship.

Crimson suffered a serious abdominal wound protecting her from assassins. It still causes severe pain. Evelyn's family guards a sacred artifact that can turn people into liches.

The search for Evelyn

A powerful drow leader defeated them at Ravenwood Manor and abducted Evelyn. Crimson calls him the Evil Drow King. Evelyn's father ordered Crimson to rescue her and bring back her captor, keeping her disappearance secret.

With repaired armor and a longsword replacing his broken greatsword, Crimson left the manor. Zaythamore offered information on the drow in exchange for company on a voyage to an island rumored to hold treasure and dragons.

Source: LET ME LARP.

Campaign notes

Crimson defeated Lysethia in a spar with the party's help. He and Phyllarcice later suffered nightmares linked to their lycanthropy while fleeing Redwood.
