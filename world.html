World & map

Based on the campaign map and General Info.

The United Cities of Feloa

Southeastern towns and cities ruled by an elected High Council whose members serve for life. Their trade network spans the continent, exporting crops, clothing, paper, and luxury goods. Humans, elves, halflings, and dwarves live here.

  • Magic users and items must be registered. Public magic is tightly controlled, with university or guard sponsorship central to practicing it.
  • The military fields mages and golems. Some young mages leave to become adventurers.
  • Gods of fortune and harvest are widely worshipped. Worship outside the main pantheon carries a death sentence.

The Tristinian dynasty

In the far south, mostly human fiefdoms fight over descent from King Tristan, who is said to have united the continent five centuries ago. Cyrus Vindhelm III claims the throne and wants the territories brought into line.

Grisem's Teeth

An underground dwarven civilization across the continent's largest mountain range. It exports tools, weapons, and precious metals while remaining neutral. The terrain and defenses have kept attackers away for hundreds of years.

Drow Kingdom

A ruler devoted to profane ritual and despotic rule steadily expands the kingdom's borders.

From our sessions: Phusgloth is in the north, allied with the Redwoods and hobgoblins.

Other territories

Minor lords and organizations hold the remaining lands. Some keep their size and influence hidden.

Places from Crimson's history

From LET ME LARP; these places are not labeled on the sketch.

Winterfall City
A northern kingdom surrounded by forest and small villages. Worship centers on Tyr, god of law and justice, and even minor offenses draw severe punishment. Crimson served the Ravenwood family here.
Bjorgvin
Winter Crow's snow-elf tribe, a day's walk into the forest. The tribe worships Shevarash and lives peacefully with Winterfall. Winter Crow raised Crimson here after the attack on his village.
Drobro Kingdom
The nearby drow realm named in Crimson's backstory. His account does not name Evelyn's captor as Phusgloth.
